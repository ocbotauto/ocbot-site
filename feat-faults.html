Recovery from drops — the bot diagnoses and fixes

In plain words: a "drop" is when a card instantly quits mining (the hashrate tanks or the card disappears). The bot unfolds the event: cause, boundary, clean recovery.

How it works

  1. A drop and Xid — in plain words

    An NVIDIA driver error is printed in the Hive log as "Xid number" — the card honestly admits it could not continue. Causes are few: the overclock was beyond its limit, the driver got wedged, the memory got hot. The bot reads the Xid and matches it to the card's current preset.

  2. First reaction — a safe profile

    As soon as a card hangs or turns into a constant failure, the bot puts safe values on it — the rig keeps running. A Telegram notice explains the cause and the actions.

  3. Escalating actions on a hang

    If the profile did not help, the bot reboots the rig itself, up to 3 attempts. If that also fails, the reboot is delayed to let the system settle and is checked again later. Manual intervention is unnecessary: recovery is fully automatic.

  4. A boundary — a lesson kept, not lost

    After a drop the bot records: "this card has no point above this line". The boundary is per-card. Not to confuse: if the card simply slowed because of the power budget — the bot changes the preset instead of rebooting the rig.

What you see

A Telegram message per drop: which card, what happened (the Xid code), what the bot did (safe profile or reboot), where the boundary is now. History — /history 3. You can also mark manually: /faulty 3 — consider the card faulty and stop its automation; /faulty 3 off — return it to work.

FAQ

Does one card drop take the whole rig down?

No: the safe profile is for the card responsible only; the rest keep their presets. A full reboot is an escalated step, not the first reaction.

What happens to a "problem" card?

If repeats survive 3 attempts, protection kicks in: after 5–7 hits in a row the bot returns the last good preset and stops stressing the card. You see it in Telegram.

Do I reboot the rig after a drop?

No: the bot does it itself. Manually — only if you prefer.

What do drop notifications show?

The Xid code, the rig, the card and the actions — the whole history is stored, so any night is easy to recall.
