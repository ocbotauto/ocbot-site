What OCBot never asks for

ItemThe truth
Seed phrase / wallet keysNever requested — OCBot does not connect to wallets and cannot move funds. There is simply nothing for it there.
HiveOS / phone / bank passwordsNot requested. The bot stores only its local config file and the Telegram bot token that you personally create.
Saved cards / auto-paymentsPayments are manual only, USDT (BEP20) to the address you see on the Buy page. No payment profiles, no recurring billing.
Hidden minersThe reverse: OCBot does not mine at all. It only tunes the overclock of the miner you installed yourself in HiveOS.

What it actually does

OCBot lives on your farm (in your HiveOS) and talks only to you through your own Telegram bot:

  • finds and locks per-card overclock presets (core / mem / Power Limit within your PSU limits), verifies stability before saving, keeps a profile per card;
  • watches for degradation: temperatures, dead cards, hashrate drops; helps recover after faults;
  • reports: what changed, what was saved, what needs your attention.

Payments go to the wallet address shown on the Buy page; the license key is issued by the same sales worker at the moment of payment.

Verifiability

The buyer build is obfuscated (no comments or docstrings) and protected by a per-rig license key — the bot's sources are not published. Before installing you can verify the build with the distribution's verify_protected.py against the public build archive: ocbot-dist. Questions: Telegram support bot — answered by a human (us), no middle-bots.

Payments are non-refundable: instead there is a free 3-day trial before buying. Licenses renew manually on the Buy page; no subscriptions, no auto-charges.

Small project: simple terms, manual payment, questions straight to Telegram. Nothing to hide: the build is verifiable, no trackers or hidden charges.
