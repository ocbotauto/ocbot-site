How to connect

  1. VK

    Status: planned. Notifications over VK messages — the same single /notify command as the other channels.

  2. Email

    Status: planned. An email with the same notification text — handy as an archive channel and for slow events.

  3. SMS / calls

    Status: planned. Most interesting for critical events (rig unreachable, zero hash on all cards) — a call or SMS when nothing was read.

Getting notified first

The bot checks for a new version once a day and offers /update in Telegram — new channels appear in the /notify list right after the update, with the connect hint in /help.

Feedback and channel priority requests — straight in the support chat.

FAQ

Can VK/Email/SMS be set up today?

Not yet: those channels are not implemented, so /notify does not list them. This page describes the plan and delivery order.

How do new channels arrive?

One per release; the connect flow is always the same: /notify + hints — you only provide the address or token.

Terms: connect with /notify …, test with /notify test, disable with /notify off …; full terms and disclaimer are in the guide.
