Compromise mode — the default

In plain words: the bot picks maximum hashrate among points where efficiency (MH/s per watt) is no worse than 97% of the best. The "golden middle" — that is why it is the default.

How it works

  1. What changes

    The bot takes the fastest point among those losing no more than 3% by economy. In practice — almost maximum hashrate at almost the best efficiency.

  2. Why it is the default

    Because the balance rarely loses: speed is near maximum, electricity is barely overpaid. A beginner sets up nothing — it is already on.

  3. How to enable/check

    In Telegram: /mode compromise — enable; /mode — show the current rig goal; /mode 3 compromise — set one card. The 3% tolerance is a fixed comparison rule, not a hand knob.

  4. What about verification

    As always: new points pass the 2-hour check before saving (see the 2-hour verification).

What "within 3%" means

Say the best economic point yields 0.60 MH/W. Compromise candidates must be no worse than 0.58 MH/W; from those the bot takes the fastest. The card ends up both fast and economical.

FAQ

How much do I lose versus raw speed?

Usually 1–3% of hashrate versus the hashrate mode, with a visible saving in watts. Your tariff decides: /table shows both numbers.

Can I raise the tolerance?

The tolerance is fixed inside the bot (the comparison rule) — it is not meant to be edited manually. For other goals there are separate modes.

What if I want raw maximum?

That is /mode hashrate — see its page. Switching on the fly is fine.
